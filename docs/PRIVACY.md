# FoodnRep Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

## 1. Introduction

FoodnRep is a food and exercise tracking application designed to help users record their food intake, monitor their exercise activities, and maintain a basic wellness profile.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FoodnRep handles, how that information is used, which third-party services may process it, and how users can contact us about their privacy.

FoodnRep is operated by **Zeyhtird Del Rosario Gomez**, based in the Philippines.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you use FoodnRep, the application may process the following information:

- **Account information:** Your name, email address, account identifier, email-verification status, and account timestamps.
- **Wellness information:** Your height, weight, and calculated body mass index (BMI).
- **Food journal entries:** Food names, quantities in grams, calorie information, and entry timestamps.
- **Exercise journal entries:** Exercise names, repetitions, sets, weights, duration, categories, and entry timestamps.
- **Technical information:** API request methods and URLs recorded in server logs, along with technical information that service providers may generate when operating their services.

The precise technical information collected may depend on the application's configuration and the services used.

FoodnRep is intended for individuals aged 18 and older. The current application code reviewed for this policy does not record a separate age-confirmation or Terms-acceptance record.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use information handled by FoodnRep to:

- Create and authenticate your account.
- Maintain your wellness profile.
- Calculate BMI based on your height and weight.
- Record and display food intake and exercise activities.
- Generate food and exercise summaries.
- Retrieve nutrition information when you request an automatic food lookup.
- Retrieve exercise information based on your selected filters.
- Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the application.

FoodnRep is a tracking tool and is not intended to provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical advice.

## 4. Third-Party Services

FoodnRep uses, or contains integrations with, third-party services that support its functionality.

These include:

- **Firebase Authentication (Google):** Used for account registration, sign-in, email verification, and authentication.
- **MongoDB:** Used to store application records, including wellness profiles and food and exercise entries.
- **Vercel:** Identified in the repository configuration for frontend delivery; its current production use has not been independently verified.
- **Render:** Referenced in the repository's backend deployment instructions; its current production use has not been independently verified.
- **CalorieAPI:** Receives food-name queries when automatic nutrition lookup is requested.
- **API Ninjas:** Receives exercise-library filters to retrieve exercise information.

When you request information from an external nutrition or exercise service, relevant search terms or filters may be sent to that provider to fulfill your request.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and service terms. Their actual processing locations, retention practices, and data-handling arrangements depend on the services and configurations in use.

## 5. Cookies, Browser Storage, and Analytics

The application source code reviewed for this policy did not contain a direct Google Analytics integration or direct calls to browser cookie, local-storage, or session-storage APIs.

However, Firebase Authentication or other services may use browser storage or similar technologies to maintain your sign-in session. Actual behavior depends on the deployed configuration.

Google Analytics was not enabled in the inspected application source. This does not independently establish whether other tracking technologies are present in the production environment.
